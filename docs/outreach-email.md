**Subject:** A free one-hour AI safety workshop for your staff

Hi [name],

I'm [one line: who you are and how you know the organization, e.g. "a member at ..." or "a
student at ... who volunteers with ..."]. I've put together a free one-hour workshop that
teaches nonprofit staff and volunteers to use AI tools like Claude carefully: getting useful
drafts without made-up facts, checking numbers before they go to a funder or client, keeping
client information out, and knowing when not to use it.

Would [organization] be open to hosting it for [your staff / your volunteers / a few people]?
It takes an hour, works with 4 to 20 people, and needs a room with a screen. It's free, there's
no client information involved (every example is made up), and it isn't selling anything.

The one thing I'd ask is a short, anonymous quiz at the start and the end, so I can tell if the
hour actually helped.

Everything is online if you'd like to look first:
https://nathanaelhub.github.io/nonprofit-ai-workshop/ (there's a one-page summary at
https://nathanaelhub.github.io/nonprofit-ai-workshop/host.html).

Would a time in the next few weeks work? Happy to talk by phone first.

Thanks,
[Your name]
[Phone or email]

## Who to ask

Start where you already know someone; a warm introduction gets a yes far more often than a cold
email. Good first hosts are small: a church staff or volunteer team, a campus service
organization, a food pantry, a tutoring program. Ask the person who runs staff or volunteer
meetings; an hour often fits into one.

## After they say yes

Follow the facilitator guide's "A week before" list. Afterward, add the results to `pilots/`

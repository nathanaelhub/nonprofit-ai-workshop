# Exercise 3: Protect clients

**10 minutes. Work with a partner. Don't paste these emails into Claude.**

You answer the email for a small nonprofit that helps people pay utility bills. Three emails came
in this morning. Everyone and everything in them is made up.

**Email 1**

> **From:** Marisol Ventura &lt;mventura@example.org&gt;
>
> Hi, my name is Marisol Ventura and I live at 418 Larkin Ave, Apt 3. My light bill is two months
> behind and they say they will shut it off on the 14th. My account number is 5527-90114. I have
> two kids, Leo (6) and Ana (3), and Ana has asthma so we need the power for her breathing machine.
> My birthday is 3/9/1991 if you need it. Please help.

**Email 2**

> **From:** Dale Whitcomb &lt;dwhitcomb@example.org&gt;
>
> I got out of the hospital last week after heart surgery and I can't work for a while. I'm behind
> on my gas bill by $286. My caseworker at the county is Ms. Okafor. My phone is 555-0142.

**Email 3**

> **From:** Grace Lindqvist &lt;glindqvist@example.org&gt;
>
> My water was shut off yesterday at 77 Orchard Lane. I lost my job at the plant in September. I
> live with my mom who is 81. What can I do?

## Step 1: find the private details (4 minutes)

Use a highlighter or circle them. Look for:

- names (of anyone, including kids and caseworkers)
- addresses, phone numbers, emails, account numbers
- birthdays and ages
- health details
- job or money details about a person

## Step 2: ask without them (4 minutes)

You want Claude to help you write a reply you can use for all three. Write a request that has
**none** of the details you found, but still tells Claude enough to help.

Hint: describe the kind of problem, not the person. For example, "someone whose power will be shut
off soon" instead of a name and an address.

## Step 3: talk it over (2 minutes)

Did Claude still help? What did you have to add yourself before you could send a reply?

**What to take away:** Claude can help with the shape of the work without anyone's private
details. You add the person's details yourself, in your own email or case system, not in Claude.

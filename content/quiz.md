# Quick quiz

No names. Circle one answer for each question. It's fine to guess.

**Before** or **After** the workshop? (circle one)

**1. You need a paragraph for a grant report. What helps Claude most?**

- A. Asking it to "write something good"
- B. Telling it who will read it, how long it should be, and what facts to use
- C. Asking it to use big words
- D. Asking it many times until one sounds right

**2. Claude tells you an income limit for a program. What do you do before you tell a client?**

- A. Nothing. Claude is usually right.
- B. Ask Claude if it is sure.
- C. Find the number on the agency's own page, and check what dates it is for.
- D. Ask a coworker if it sounds right.

**3. Two good sources disagree about a program rule. What do you tell the client?**

- A. Pick the one that helps the client most.
- B. Tell them they don't qualify, to be safe.
- C. Tell them to apply anyway, and call the agency or 211 to find out.
- D. Ask Claude which one is right.

**4. Which of these is OK to type into Claude?**

- A. A client's name and address
- B. A client's case number
- C. "A parent whose power will be shut off this week"
- D. A photo of a client's ID

**5. What happens when you click thumbs up or down on a chat?**

- A. Nothing leaves your computer.
- B. Only your rating is sent.
- C. The whole chat is sent to Anthropic, and it may be kept for up to 5 years.
- D. The chat is deleted.

**6. You pasted a client's email into Claude by mistake. What do you do first?**

- A. Nothing. It was only once.
- B. Delete the chat, and tell the person who handles privacy at work that day.
- C. Ask Claude to forget it.
- D. Close the browser.

**7. Which task should Claude NOT do?**

- A. Write a thank-you note to a volunteer
- B. Make a flyer easier to read
- C. Decide if a family qualifies for rent help
- D. List ideas for a fundraiser

**8. Someone texts your office that they want to hurt themselves. What should happen?**

- A. Ask Claude to write a kind reply.
- B. A person responds right away and follows your crisis plan.
- C. Send them a link to a website.
- D. Reply tomorrow during office hours.

**Reading the results honestly.** With a small group, a change of one or two people is noise.
A question many people got right *before* the workshop is too easy to show anything; a question
fewer people got right *after* means that part of the hour isn't working, so tell the maintainer.
The quiz measures what people know at the end of an hour, not what they'll do next month.

# Using Claude at a Nonprofit

A one-hour workshop for nonprofit staff who don't write code. It teaches four habits:

1. **Ask well.** Give Claude the context, the audience and the facts to use.
2. **Check before using.** Find the source for any number or claim before it goes in a report.
3. **Protect clients.** Keep names, birthdays, case details and anything identifying out of Claude.
4. **Know when not to use it.** Decisions about a person's eligibility, safety or legal standing
   stay with people.
